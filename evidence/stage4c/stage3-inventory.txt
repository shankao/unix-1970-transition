Stage 3 KA11 instruction inventory (local-source audit)

Sources audited:
  tools/build_stage3a.py
  tools/build_stage3b.py
  src/pdp11/threaded-b/core.s
  src/pdp11/threaded-b/control-call.s

Unique mnemonics used by the proven Stage 3 builders:
  add asl asr bne bpl br clr cmp halt jmp mov movb tst tstb

Byte variants used:
  movb tstb

Branches used:
  br bne bpl

Addressing forms used:
  register
  register deferred
  autoincrement
  autoincrement deferred
  autodecrement
  indexed
  immediate (PC autoincrement)
  absolute (PC autoincrement deferred)

The Stage 3 sources do not use autodecrement deferred, indexed deferred,
ordinary PC-relative operands, JSR, RTS, or raw assembler-word statements as
PDP-11 instructions. Stage 4C nevertheless tests all eight KA11 operand modes;
JSR and RTS are included as cheap bootstrap-relevant members of encoding
classes already needed by the gate.

The Stage 3 threaded operator named `mark` is a data-stream symbol, not the
later PDP-11 MARK instruction.
